# Machine-in-a-weekend

## Summary
Ilan Moyer, Leo McElroy, and Jake Read organized a machine building workshop over a weekend in October 2024, during this time we were tasked with building a machine mechanism. What followed was a mad dash to build this robotic arm that relies on a standard SCARA architecture, with a neat coaxial transmission method. I built this mostly from scrap stock, and was pleasantly surprised by the outcome.
